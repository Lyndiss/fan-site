Ampharos

Ampharos is an Electric-type Pokémon known for its bright,
glowing tail and gentle nature. Its light can shine from great distances,
making it useful for guiding travelers at night.
Ampharos is calm and friendly, but it can unleash
powerful electric attacks when it needs to defend itself.
